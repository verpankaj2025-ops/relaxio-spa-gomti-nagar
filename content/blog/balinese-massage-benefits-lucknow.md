Balinese Massage vs Thai Massage

Thai Massage is more focused on assisted stretching, guided movement and structured pressure.

Balinese Massage is generally more relaxation-oriented and often includes aromatherapy.

Choose Balinese when:

Relaxation is your main goal

You prefer flowing massage movements

Aromatherapy appeals to you

Choose Thai when:

You prefer stretching

You want mobility-focused bodywork

You like a more structured session
