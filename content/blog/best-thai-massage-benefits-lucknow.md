## What is Thai Massage?

Thai massage is a traditional wellness therapy that combines assisted stretching, acupressure techniques, and rhythmic body movements to improve flexibility and relaxation.

Unlike standard oil massages, Thai massage focuses on body balance, energy flow, and muscle recovery. This therapy is widely recommended for people experiencing stress, body fatigue, muscle stiffness, and mental exhaustion.
