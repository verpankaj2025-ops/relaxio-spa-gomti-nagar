What Should You Check Before Booking a Spa?

Price is only one part of the decision. Before booking, compare:

1. Session duration

A 60-minute session and a 90-minute session should not be compared only by total price. Check the duration and the treatment included.

2. Massage type

Thai, Balinese and Deep Tissue Massage offer different wellness experiences. Choose the treatment that matches your preferred pressure and style.

3. Package inclusions

Some packages include facilities such as shower, steam or other add-ons. Check the inclusions before confirming the booking.

4. Private environment

For couples and guests who value privacy, the room setup and overall environment can matter as much as the price.

5. Current availability

Package prices and availability can change. Confirm the latest details with Relaxio Spa before visiting.
